# Audio credits

- The Fallout Forecast theme, bass pulse, storm and boss layers, and airdrop touchdown are original synthesized audio. Regenerate them with `tools/audio/gen_forecast_score.py`.
- `thunder.wav` and `thunder_web.wav` use [big thunder clap by seth-m](https://freesound.org/people/seth-m/sounds/458015/) ([CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)). The recording was trimmed, faded, and high-pass filtered. The Web mix is mono with more midrange for small speakers.
- Sound effects by Kenney (CC0). [Kenney assets](https://kenney.nl/assets)
- Firearm reports, item handling, and weapon deployment and blast cues are original synthesized audio. Regenerate them with `tools/audio/gen_gun_sfx.py` and `tools/audio/gen_inventory_sfx.py`.
- Rain, wind, the gated Downpour drum, weather bulletins, infected warnings, shield dome and decon spray synthesized for Fallout Forecast. Regenerate the storm and hazard cues with `tools/audio/gen_storm_sfx.py`.
- Cassette transport sounds synthesized for Fallout Forecast. Regenerate them with `tools/audio/gen_vcr_sfx.py`.
- Team ping radio cues synthesized for Fallout Forecast. Regenerate them with `tools/audio/gen_ping_sfx.py`.
- Weather infected and incoming-group cues synthesized for Fallout Forecast. Regenerate them with `tools/audio/gen_weather_enemy_sfx.py`.
